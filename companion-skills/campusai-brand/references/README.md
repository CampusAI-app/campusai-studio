# References

Real examples, good and bad. Look at these before designing anything. Newest at the top of each
list. Each line says the one thing to take or avoid. Add new ones the same way (see "Adding to the
brand" in `../SKILL.md`).

## Good

- **`good/2026-10-16-dashboard-laptop.jpg`** (Jacob, Oct 16). The look to match: the real
  dashboard on the near-black ground, one red glow behind it, the product big enough to read.
  The phone that sat next to it in the original was cropped out, because it showed Testudo and
  campus photos.

## Bad

- **`bad/2026-10-28-chatgpt-linkedin-banner.jpg`** (Aiden, Oct 28, made by ChatGPT). Nothing in it
  is ours: navy blue instead of red-black, "CampusAI" retyped in a generic font with no spark,
  a generated campus building at night (no campus imagery, ever), a generic tagline ("the
  student operating system for higher education"), a short blue bar under the headline, and the
  left half empty. It is what any tool makes with no brand loaded. The fix is in
  `../templates/linkedin-banner.html`.
